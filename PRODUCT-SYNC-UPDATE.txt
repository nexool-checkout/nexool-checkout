SECURE CHECKOUT 0.3.0 - OPTIONAL SHOPIFY TO WHOP PRODUCT SYNC

Open https://checkout.nexool.shop/setup and sign in with your ADMIN_SECRET.
Under "4. Shopify -> Whop product sync", turn the switch On, then choose
"Import / refresh all products" once for the current Shopify catalog.
The switch defaults to Off. No new environment variable is required.
The first switch change creates the needed Neon tables automatically.

ON
Registers Shopify products/create, products/update and products/delete webhooks.
Imports active products in batches and saves each Shopify ID -> Whop ID mapping.
New or renamed products are fetched from Shopify and synced automatically.
Repeated events and imports reuse mappings; names are not used as identities.
Products are scoped to the Shopify shop and Whop business, including when two
stores have identical names or use the same Whop business.
Whop products start hidden and do not send digital-product welcome messages.
Titles/descriptions sync; image galleries and Whop marketplace approval do not.
Whop product titles are limited to 80 characters; payment plan titles to 30.
The existing checkout product's tax category is inherited when it has one.
Review categories in Whop when different Shopify products require different tax
treatment. Existing total, tax and currency matching checks remain enforced.

OFF
Stops starting new product-sync work and stops selecting synced products for
new checkout preparation. Checkout uses WHOP_PRODUCT_ID / WHOP_RECEIPT_TITLE.
Products and historical orders are retained. A request already running may finish.
Turning the switch off does not change a quote/payment already prepared.
Turn it back on and refresh the catalog to catch edits made while it was off.

CHECKOUT
A cart containing one Shopify product (including several of its variants) uses
that product's synced Whop ID once the mapping is ready.
Until ready, and for mixed-product carts, the existing whole-cart Whop product
is used, with Shopify item names, variants and quantities in the description.
Mixed carts are not split into separate charges. Full order items remain in
Shopify, and Whop payments retain the Shopify draft/session reference.
Whop's newer SDK also supports cart line_items; this release retains the existing
whole-cart payment flow for mixed carts. A separate change would be needed to
price and validate those itemized Whop carts with Shopify discounts and shipping.

BULK PROGRESS AND RETRIES
Keep the setup page open during bulk importing. Progress is saved in Neon.
After closing the page, use "Continue sync" to resume. Failed items show a private
error and can be retried. The existing daily reconciliation also advances the
queue; Shopify product webhooks process the changed product after delivery.
An ambiguous create older than 23 hours is held for review rather than risking a
duplicate after Whop's 24-hour idempotency window.

PERMISSIONS
Shopify needs the existing read_products permission and product-topic webhooks.
The Whop API key must be allowed to read, create and update products.
Credentials and webhook secrets remain in Vercel; never add them to GitHub.

APPLE PAY / OLD WEBHOOK LOGS
The domain.created event dated 2026-10-25 contains the state from creation time.
An old payload's action_required does not describe a newly verified domain.
Whop Websites is where to check the domain's current Apple Pay status.
A real delivery failure is identified by its HTTP response/status, not by the
old apple_pay.state inside a historical event. The verified company_id/account_id
v1 compatibility handler and signature validation remain unchanged.
