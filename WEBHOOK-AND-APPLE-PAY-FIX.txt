SECURE CHECKOUT 0.2.2 - WEBHOOK AND APPLE PAY DOMAIN FIX

CONFIRMED FROM YOUR PAYLOAD
The business ID is correct. Your signed webhook is v1 without a date pin, so its
top-level account field is company_id. The previous handler expected account_id
and returned Wrong account before processing or acknowledging the event.
The updated handler accepts either documented v1 field after verifying the
signature. It still rejects missing, different and conflicting business IDs.
Payment processing still re-fetches the payment from Whop and checks the account,
session, quote, total, currency and refund state before completing a Shopify order.
Signed events other than payment.succeeded are acknowledged and ignored.

Apple Pay's separate reported state is action_required / apple_pay_unverified.
Whop says the verification file could not be fetched from your checkout domain.
This package adds Whop's officially documented Vercel rewrite for that file.
