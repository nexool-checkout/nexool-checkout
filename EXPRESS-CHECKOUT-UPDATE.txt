SECURE CHECKOUT 0.4.0 - EARLY CARD FIELDS AND EXPRESS WALLETS

Deploy this update to the existing Vercel project. Existing environment values,
Neon data, product-sync settings and Shopify theme connection remain in use.
There are no new environment variables or database migrations.

The real Whop payment form now loads as soon as the cart is available, before
the buyer completes delivery. It remains mounted while address, shipping and
total changes update its amount/currency. Card details stay in Whop's payment
fields; the merchant application never reads or stores them.

Eligible Apple Pay and Google Pay buttons appear above Contact. Availability
comes from Whop's real account/device/domain probe, including its supplied
button artwork. Unsupported methods are hidden. No simulated availability is
used in production. The domain must remain verified in Whop Websites.

The wallet collects contact and shipping details. Its redacted address-change
events request an estimate from Shopify without inventing names, an email,
street details or postal suffixes. These estimates create no order and cannot
authorize payment. Changing the delivery country also refreshes Shopify's
market price/currency without waiting for the complete manual address.

Before approving a wallet token, the app validates the full contact/address,
gets a full Shopify quote and compares the wallet-approved amount, currency
and shipping option. It then reserves the order and rechecks the Shopify and
Whop quote before submitting the token. Existing signatures, payment ownership,
tax/amount matching and payment idempotency remain enforced.

If the full address changes the total, or the buyer selects a country with a
different market currency, the app refuses that authorization and asks the buyer
to close the wallet, review the updated checkout and retry. This avoids charging
a total or currency the buyer did not approve. If a carrier cannot quote from a
wallet-redacted address, the buyer can complete delivery on the page first.
